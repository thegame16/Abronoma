How a dress gets made

Six to eight weeks, start to finish, across five stages — every one of them with you in the room, or on a call.

1

Sketch & consultation

We talk fabric, occasion and silhouette. A sketch is drawn from your first measurements.

2

Pattern drafted

Your pattern is drafted from scratch — not graded down from a standard block.

3

Muslin fitting

A calico mock-up is cut and fitted on you, adjusted pin by pin until the shape is right.

4

Cut & hand-sewn

The pattern is cut into your chosen cloth and sewn with hand-finished seams throughout.

5

Final fitting

You try on the finished dress. We make the last, smallest adjustments — then it's yours.
