Notes from the Fitting Room

The Atelier

Every VERSO piece is cut in the same small workroom outside Biella, by a team of eleven pattern-makers who have worked together for most of two decades. We keep no more than four styles in production at once, so that every seam gets looked at twice before it ships.

We don't chase seasons. We chase the version of a coat, a shirt, or a trouser that we'd still want to wear in ten years — and then we spend the next ten years making small, careful changes to it.
